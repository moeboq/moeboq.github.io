About

A Thai civil engineer who shows you where every baht goes

Hi, I'm Moe, a civil engineer working in building design and construction in Thailand, currently studying for a master's degree in infrastructure engineering and management. I prepare Bills of Quantities and cost estimates so owners know the real budget before they commit.

Measured from your drawings

No flat rate per square metre. Every quantity comes from the drawings and can be traced in the Excel file.

Prices you can check

Materials follow the Thai Ministry of Commerce reference prices and labour follows the Comptroller General's Department rates. Estimated items are marked.

Assumptions in writing

Anything the drawings leave open is written into the BOQ notes, so you can review and change it.

Scope

This service covers quantity take-off and cost estimating from the drawings you provide. It does not include structural design or certification; for sign-off, contact the project's design engineer.
