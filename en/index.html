Why two-storey houses cost less per square metre

A two-storey house uses one roof and one set of foundations for two floors, so those costs are spread over more floor area. Even after adding stairs and an upper floor slab, a bungalow of the same size usually costs 10–15% more per square metre.

Where the money goes

Structure (foundations, columns, beams and slabs) is typically 30–40% of the cost, architectural work (walls, floors, ceilings, roof, paint, doors, windows and bathrooms) about 55–60%, and electrical and plumbing 5–8%. For bungalows, the roof is usually the single most expensive item.

Getting a reliable number

Use the calculator to set an early budget. Once you have drawings, a Bill of Quantities lists every item measured from the plans. It lets you compare contractor quotes line by line and keep the budget under control during construction.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build a house in Thailand in 2026?

Based on September 2026 Thai government reference prices for materials and labour, a standard house costs roughly ฿9,000–11,000 per m² (about $25–30 per sq ft), including a 10% contractor margin and 7% VAT. Single-storey houses cost more per m² than two-storey houses because the roof and foundations cover the whole floor area.

What is included in the estimate?

Foundations and piles, the concrete structure, walls, floor finishes, ceilings, roof, painting, doors and windows, bathroom fixtures, and basic electrical and plumbing. Land, design and permit fees, fences, landscaping, furniture, built-ins and appliances are not included.

Can a foreigner build a house in Thailand?

Foreigners generally cannot own land in Thailand, so houses are usually built on a Thai spouse's land or on leased land. Ownership rules are complex, so get independent legal advice before you build.

Why do contractor quotes differ so much?

Quotes differ by material choices, contractor margin, site conditions, and how quantities were taken off the drawings. A Bill of Quantities (BOQ) prepared from your actual drawings is the best baseline for comparing quotes.

Are prices outside Bangkok different?

The estimate uses Bangkok and vicinity prices. Labour is often cheaper in the provinces, while transport can make some materials more expensive in remote areas. Expect differences of roughly ±10%.

Where the numbers come from

  • Material prices: Thai Ministry of Commerce (TPSO) construction material prices for Bangkok and vicinity, September 2026; Office of the Basic Education Commission price list FY2026; labour rates from the Comptroller General's Department (June 2026). Some items are estimates.
  • Quantities are scaled per floor area from a sample house measured in a full BOQ. Every house differs, so the result is shown as a ±15% range.
  • Exchange rates are indicative mid-market rates from 12–13 Nov 2026 and will change.
  • Excludes land, design and permit fees, fences, external works, furniture, built-ins, appliances and transport to remote provinces. This is a budget estimate, not a quotation or a structural design.
