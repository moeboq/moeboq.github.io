Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the BOQ?

Quantities are measured from your drawings item by item. Material prices follow the latest Thai Ministry of Commerce reference prices and labour rates follow the Comptroller General's Department. You can edit any price in Excel and the BOQ recalculates.

What if my drawings are incomplete?

I will list what the drawings do not show and state the assumptions used in the BOQ notes, so you can check them.

How many revisions are included?

Two revisions in every package, for example changing materials, adding items or updating prices.

Do you certify structural designs?

No. This service covers quantity take-off and cost estimating only, not structural design or sign-off.

What language are the files in?

BOQ files are prepared in Thai, the format Thai contractors use, so you can hand them straight to local builders.
